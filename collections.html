After it leaves

Care notes we print on every lid

Chocolate blooms in a hot car faster than guests forgive — keep the demo copy, tune the temperatures for your kitchen.

  • Chocolate bars — cool, dry cupboard; avoid the fridge unless your kitchen runs warm.
  • Cream-filled tubs — refrigerate within two hours of pickup; enjoy within 48 hours.
  • Crepes & waffles — best warm from the pass; if travelling, re-crisp in a dry pan.

Savory anchors ship separately from chocolate — ask the desk for a split invoice.
