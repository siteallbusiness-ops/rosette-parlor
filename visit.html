Room rhythm

Salon etiquette we ask everyone to honour

The space is small; a few house rules keep the room calm for pastry people and first dates alike.

  • Phones on silent — quick photos welcome, flash off.
  • One laptop at a time during the quiet hour (15:00–17:00).
  • Outside cakes need a heads-up 48 hours ahead — we charge a small plating fee (demo).
  • Strollers park by the door; high chairs available on request.
